How a repair works

  1. Email us the problem, or bring the bike to the workshop during opening hours.
  2. We inspect it and send a written quote with the parts and labour listed.
  3. You approve the quote. We repair the bike and tell you when it is ready.

Most repairs are ready within three working days. If we find a second fault while we work, we stop and send a new quote before going further.
