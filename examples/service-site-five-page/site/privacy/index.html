Privacy

This website does not set cookies, run analytics or load third-party scripts. The only personal data we receive is what you put in an email to us.

What we hold

If you email workshop@example.com, we hold your email address, your name if you give it, and the details of the repair. We use them to reply, to quote and to repair your bicycle.

How long we keep it

We keep repair emails for twelve months after the repair, then delete them. We do not sell or share them.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will reply within one month.

Reporting a security problem

If you find a security problem with this website, email security@example.com. Tell us the page and what you saw. We will acknowledge your report within three working days. Please do not access other people's data while testing.
