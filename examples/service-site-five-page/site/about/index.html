About the workshop

Hillside Cycle Workshop is an independent repair shop in Example Town, run by two mechanics.

How we work

One mechanic inspects the bike and writes the quote. The same mechanic does the repair, so the person who explains the work is the person who did it.

We keep the scope narrow on purpose. We repair and service bicycles. We do not sell new bikes and we do not offer collection or delivery.

What you can hold us to

  • A written quote before work starts.
  • Your approval before any work is done.
  • Old parts returned if you ask for them.
  • A message from us before we go beyond the quote.

Opening hours and the address are on the contact page.
