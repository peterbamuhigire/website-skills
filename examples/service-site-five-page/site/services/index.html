Services

We do six jobs. We do not publish a price list because the cost depends on what the inspection finds. Every job starts with a written quote.

The six jobs

Basic service
Brake and gear adjustment, tyre pressure, chain clean and lubrication, bolt check.
Full service
The basic service plus a strip-down and clean of the drivetrain, wheel and headset checks, and a written list of worn parts.
Puncture and tyre fitting
Tube or tyre replacement. We check the rim and the inside of the tyre for the cause.
Brake and gear adjustment
Cable tension, pad alignment, indexing. Worn cables or pads are quoted separately.
Wheel truing
Straightening a buckled wheel. Done the same day when the bike arrives before 10:00.
Pre-purchase inspection
A written condition report on a second-hand bicycle, so you know the repair cost before you buy.

How we quote

  • The quote is in writing and lists parts and labour.
  • No work starts until you approve it.
  • We return the old parts if you ask.

We do not collect or deliver bicycles. Contact us to arrange a drop-off.
