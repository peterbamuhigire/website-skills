# Brief: Hillside Cycle Workshop (synthetic example)

Every fact below is fictional and exists only to give the engine a complete brief to build from. The domain is `example.com`, which is reserved for documentation. Nothing here describes a real business.

## Business
An independent bicycle repair workshop in Example Town, run by two mechanics.

## Audience and goal
Commuters and parents doing the school run within about five kilometres. The goal of the site is a booking request by email. The second goal is that a visitor knows how pricing works before they get in touch.

## Services
Basic service, full service, puncture and tyre fitting, brake and gear adjustment, wheel truing, pre-purchase inspection.

## Pricing and promises
- No public price list. A written quote follows inspection.
- No work starts until the customer approves the quote.
- Most repairs are ready within three working days. Wheel truing is done the same day when the bike arrives before 10:00.
- Old parts are returned on request.
- No collection or delivery.

## Hours and place
Monday to Friday 08:00 to 17:00, Saturday 09:00 to 13:00, closed Sunday. 14 Example Road, Example Town.

## Contact
`workshop@example.com`. No telephone number is published. There is no contact form.

## Voice
Plain, practical British English. Short sentences. No claims that the workshop cannot prove.

## Constraints
Static site, five pages, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts.

## Out of scope
Online payment, a booking calendar, a blog, photography.
