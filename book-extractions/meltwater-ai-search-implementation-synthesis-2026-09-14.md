# Meltwater AI-search claim implementation synthesis

Status: adopted on 2026-09-14 as qualified, directional evidence for website
and cross-channel content planning.

## Evidence disposition

The Meltwater and LinkedIn material reports an observational analysis of 9.5
million citations from B2B prompts across 16 categories and six named AI
products. It reports LinkedIn as a leading source in that sample, with
individual member profiles contributing more citations than Company Pages and
structured, question-led content appearing frequently among highly cited
examples. These findings are useful for hypotheses, not universal ranking
rules: the study is vendor-produced, sample-bound, and does not establish
causation, traffic, lead quality, or sales impact.

The reported figures must always retain their sample, denominator, date, and
source. The study does not justify claims that LinkedIn always outranks other
sources, that follower count is ignored, or that headings, lists, posting
frequency, freshness, or format guarantee AI citation.

## Website implementation rules

- Make each important page independently useful: state its subject, audience,
  answer, evidence, date where material, limitations, and next action.
- Build connected topic coverage across definitions, how-to guides,
  comparisons, implementation pages, FAQs, case evidence, and offer pages.
- Keep entities consistent across page copy, metadata, structured data, author
  information, internal links, and legitimate external references.
- Keep facts in crawlable HTML and use structured data only when it matches
  visible, accurate content and the current consumer's eligibility rules.
- Treat the owned website as the durable explanation and conversion hub; use
  social content as a standalone answer and distribution path, not as a
  substitute for the canonical destination.
- Earn independent corroboration legitimately through partners, professional
  directories, reviews, research, and community participation. Never create
  fake mentions, reviews, or user-generated content.
- Refresh when facts, offers, laws, prices, capabilities, or evidence change;
  record the substantive change instead of changing a date cosmetically.

## Measurement and experiment contract

Track representation, retrieval/citation observation, referral, qualified
engagement, and conversion as separate outcomes. A monthly or quarterly test
should record the exact prompt set, model/product, date, locale, answer,
target URL, cited URLs, brand-accuracy result, referral activity, and business
outcome where attribution is available. Hold topic and audience reasonably
constant when comparing author, structure, format, or distribution. One
anecdotal citation is not a strategy change.
