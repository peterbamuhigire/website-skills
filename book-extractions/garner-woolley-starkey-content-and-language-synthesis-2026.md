## Durable ideas retained

### Garner: join the journey, not just the channel

The strongest transferable idea is an outside-in operating model: a person’s
experience with an organisation begins before the website visit and continues
through search, social conversation, the owned destination, response, and
follow-up. Search, social, content, community, and measurement should therefore
share a purpose and handoff model rather than operate as isolated calendars.

Retain these operating principles:

- define the audience job, organisational purpose, voice, proof, destination, and
  outcome before choosing a channel or format;
- combine evergreen pages with genuinely timely updates, while giving each item a
  page job, owner, source, approval state, expiry or review date, and recovery
  path;
- adapt a core idea to the conventions of each destination without duplicating
  language mechanically or treating people as clicks and impressions;
- make community participation conversational, useful, and subject to house
  rules, consent, moderation, and rights checks; and
- connect content, SEO, social, sales, support, legal/compliance, and analytics
  through explicit handoffs.

### Woolley: let intent and evidence govern the keyword map

The useful SEO lesson is a disciplined loop: plan, research, implement, review.
Keywords are language labels that help people and systems understand an offer;
they are not a substitute for a real page purpose or useful answer.

Retain this method:

1. Start with the customer’s vocabulary, problem, place, offer, and decision.
2. Classify the intent and page role before collecting large keyword lists.
3. Group related language into a topic/entity map, recording ambiguity and
   disambiguating context.
4. Keep a resource file with the term, source, date, locale, intent, page,
   proof burden, owner, and next review.
5. Triangulate language from customer evidence, Search Console or equivalent
   first-party data, live search observations, competitor discovery, and
   keyword tools; record what each source can and cannot establish.
6. Review qualified actions and reader usefulness, not rankings or traffic in
   isolation. Change one meaningful variable at a time where practical.

### Bishop/Starkey: distinctive voice is a quality control

The creative-writing reference provides a useful editorial counterweight to
mechanical SEO. Reader-friendly does not mean bland or faceless. Voice emerges
from deliberate choices of words, syntax, rhythm, mood, tone, point of view, and
the intelligence or values a text makes recognisable.

Apply this carefully to website content:

- define a small set of voice commitments and anti-commitments, then test them
  against real audience language and the brand’s evidence;
- use plain language and clear structure without sanding away specificity,
  local texture, or a defensible point of view;
- use editorial review to improve clarity, accuracy, inclusion, and usefulness,
  not to make every contributor sound identical;
- treat imitation and templates as learning scaffolds, never as permission to
  mimic another organisation or manufacture personality; and
- keep uncertainty, disagreement, and limits visible when they matter to the
  reader’s decision.

## Website engine application

For a page or content cluster, combine the three lenses into this brief:

| Layer | Required decision | Evidence or gate |
|---|---|---|
| Journey | Where did the reader come from, what job are they doing now, and what is the safe next action? | Journey map, route, CTA, failure path |
| Language | Which customer terms, synonyms, entities, places, and ambiguities must be understood? | Dated resource file and intent map |
| Value | What original proof, explanation, experience, or decision support does the page add? | Claim-proof map and author/owner |
| Voice | What should be recognisably human and specific without obscuring the answer? | Voice notes, read-aloud/edit pass, accessibility review |
| Distribution | Which owned, earned, or authorised social/community path can help a real person discover it? | Rights, moderation, canonical-link, and approval checks |
| Learning | What will be measured separately for visibility, referral, qualified action, and revenue? | Instrumentation map, baseline, guardrail, and review date |

This strengthens the existing AI-search doctrine without creating an AI-only
content format. Current Google guidance remains the authority: make content
helpful, reliable, people-first, crawlable, and understandable; use the words
readers use in prominent, natural locations; avoid keyword stuffing and do not
assume a preferred word count or special AI markup. See the current [Google
Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials), [SEO
Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide),
and [AI Search guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

## Claims quarantined as historical or unassessed

Do not carry forward without a new primary-source check:

- the named network roster, interface steps, product availability, or analytics
  screens in the older books;
- claims that likes, shares, velocity, or a particular social network directly
  produce Google rankings or AI citations;
- fixed keyword counts, density, head/body/long-tail volume assumptions, or
  universal conversion promises;
- the books’ references to specific algorithms, acronyms, ranking updates, or
  retired analytics products as if they were current operating rules; and
- any benchmark, statistic, platform rule, local fact, legal claim, or rights
  assertion without a current authoritative source.

## Kaizen experiment

Hypothesis: a page brief that joins journey stage, intent language, original
proof, distinctive voice, distribution permissions, and separate outcome
measures will produce fewer generic pages and clearer handoffs than a keyword
list or AI-search checklist alone.

Pilot: apply the brief to one real page or cluster. Record the baseline, source
map, content change, approval state, and route. Measure search visibility or
citation observation separately from referral, qualified action, and revenue.
Guardrails are no fabricated proof, no rights or privacy breach, no keyword
stuffing, no manufactured mentions, and no accessibility regression. Stop and
roll back if evidence becomes contradictory, the page job is unclear, or a
material claim cannot be sourced. Standardise only after the owner reviews the
result and the re-audit date is recorded.
