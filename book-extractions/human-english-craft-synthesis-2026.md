# Human English and Reader-Centred Craft — Website Content

Status: operational reference, 2026-08-27

Original synthesis only; no raw book text is stored.

## Source limits and usable lessons

The Bolitho, Mann, and *Natural English Reading & Writing Skills* files are title-only stubs and cannot support substantive attribution. David’s ceramic manual supports observation, controlled description, consistent classification, and evidence before interpretation, not website tone. Gupta’s vocabulary book supports selective word choice and confusable-word checks, not rare-word stuffing. Pinnacle’s error practice supports checks for agreement, articles, tense, prepositions, pronouns, parallelism, modifiers, punctuation, spelling, and cohesion, while exam keys may simplify usage. Betsis and Mamas’s CAE models support genre, audience, register, task coverage, paragraphing, and recommendation; they are not reusable page templates.

## Website writing standard

Write for a visitor who has arrived with a question, worry, comparison, or task. Human website copy is specific, useful, and easy to act on. It earns trust by saying what is true, what is included, what happens next, and what the organisation does not promise.

### Five passes

1. Name audience, page job, search/arrival intent, emotional temperature, and next action.
2. Build the page spine: orientation; problem or need; offer; proof; process; objections; next step; state copy.
3. Verify claims, testimonials, prices, locations, credentials, links, accessibility promises, and SEO entities.
4. Revise headings, paragraphs, CTA labels, microcopy, grammar, rhythm, and scan order; keep proof next to claims.
5. Proof on desktop and mobile contexts, read aloud, skim headings, and check error, empty, loading, confirmation, and privacy states.

## Craft rules

- British English and East African defaults apply unless the brief names another market.
- Prefer exact common words. Use advanced vocabulary only when its meaning, connotation, collocation, and register are right.
- Check agreement, articles, tense, pronouns, relative clauses, prepositions, parallelism, modifiers, punctuation, spelling, and sentence boundaries.
- Replace vague praise with a service detail, result with evidence, process, limit, comparison, or qualification.
- Give a page and paragraph one clear job. Vary rhythm naturally; do not fill every section with the same three-item pattern.
- CTA text should name the action and, where useful, the expected result: “Request a stock check” is stronger than “Learn more”.
- Do not add typos, fake colloquialisms, or inflated warmth to simulate a person.

## Anti-slop questions

Could this page belong to ten unrelated companies? Add the real service, audience, place, process, proof, boundary, or next action—or cut it. Does the first screen orient the visitor? Do headings remain useful when scanned alone? Are claims honest and close to evidence? Are states covered? Do not let SEO structure replace a good argument.

## Evidence record

Record page job, audience, source/proof map, market and language, terminology checks, state-copy review, read-aloud/proof status, unresolved gaps, reviewer, and date.
