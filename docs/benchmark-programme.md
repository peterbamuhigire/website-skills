# Benchmark Programme

## Acceptance rule

The engine may claim 95+ only after three production-equivalent packs pass the same 15-stage release contract and independent reviewers sign the evidence. Repository fixtures test repeatability; they are not client-outcome evidence.

## Required packs

1. East African B2B service site.
2. English/French/Kiswahili local-business site with native-review records.
3. Retail-commerce site with catalogue, cart, checkout mock, product markup, consent-aware analytics and threat review.

Each pack includes an approved brief, source register, six v1 core artefacts, source, rendered mobile/desktop baselines, machine reports, manual accessibility and screen-reader records, native-language review where relevant, analytics verification, handover, and a 30-day learning record.

## Independent review

The author cannot approve their own pack. Named reviewers cover design, accessibility, language, security/privacy, editorial/claims, and client acceptance. Reviewers calibrate on one shared sample before scoring; disagreements of more than five points require a recorded reconciliation. Legal review is performed only by a qualified reviewer for the target jurisdiction.

## Publication and maintenance

Obtain written consent before publishing client material, testimonials, analytics, or screenshots. Anonymised packs retain the verification trail. Refresh technical evidence quarterly and outcome evidence at 30 and 90 days. Expired evidence lowers the score; it is not silently carried forward.
