## Summary

Added comprehensive anti-AI writing enforcement and page-by-page copywriting formulas across all content-generating skills. Goal: every piece of generated content sounds 100% human-written with zero AI traces.

## New Files Created

### `blog-writer/references/human-voice-standards.md` (318 lines)

Central anti-AI reference. All content skills reference this file.

- **AI Vocabulary Blacklist** — 3 tiers: Tier 1 (20 instant AI tells, banned completely), Tier 2 (20 overused words, use sparingly), Tier 3 (combination rules, max 1 per paragraph)
- **AI Phrase Blacklist** — 30+ banned phrases in 4 categories (openings, filler, hype, structural)
- **AI Structural Pattern Bans** — burstiness rules, paragraph variation, list variation, transition bans, em dash limits
- **Human Voice Techniques** — take positions, mine client language, strategic contractions (2-4/500 words), sensory detail, imperfection markers, write-then-cut
- **Voice DNA Extraction** — structured process for profiling an author's voice from existing writing before first article
- **Human Voice Self-Check** — 5 verification questions

### `page-builder/references/website-copywriting.md` (264 lines)

Page-by-page copy formulas for page-builder skill.

- **Universal Principles** — headlines sell, specificity ladder, client language mining, one message per section, so-what filter
- **Homepage** — 10-second test, hero structure with mirror technique, section progression
- **About Page** — you-before-me rule, team bio guidance
- **Services Page** — outcome-first, pain-solution-proof per service, pricing signals
- **Contact Page** — barrier removal, warm invitation, WhatsApp inclusion
- **Testimonials** — specific over glowing, story structure
- **FAQ** — real questions, objection handling
- **CTA Writing** — specific CTAs, consistent wording, no manipulative language
