# Human Voice & Copywriting Enhancement — Design Doc

**Date:** 2026-03-01
**Goal:** Make all generated website content sound 100% human-written with zero AI traces.

## Problem

AI-generated content has recognisable patterns: predictable vocabulary (delve, landscape, tapestry), uniform sentence rhythm, hedging tone, and structural tells (three-part lists, "Not only X but also Y"). Current skills address marketing hype but not AI-specific tells. Page-builder lacks copywriting depth.

## Research Sources

- itsjillwise.com — reader-first copy, sales pipeline integration
- flux-academy.com — client language mining, headlines-first, write-then-cut
- Wikipedia Signs of AI Writing — 100+ vocabulary/phrase/structural tells
- AiSDR, ivanacirkovic.com — categorised AI word blacklists with replacements
- Web search on burstiness, opinion injection, sensory detail
