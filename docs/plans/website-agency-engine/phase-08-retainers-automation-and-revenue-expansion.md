# Phase 8 - Retainers, Automation, and Revenue Expansion

## Objective

Move the agency from one-time project income to a more durable revenue model that combines premium
builds with retainers, optimisation services, and selected automation.

## Why This Phase Matters

A $10k website agency becomes meaningfully stronger when it can also capture:

- maintenance revenue
- SEO and content retainers
- CRO and iteration retainers
- analytics and reporting retainers
- premium support or growth packages

This phase converts completed projects into compounding business relationships.

## Core Outcomes

- define post-launch service packages
- automate low-value repetitive work
- standardize reporting and performance reviews
- create upsell paths that increase client lifetime value

## Workstreams

### 1. Productize retainers

Create clear retainer packages such as:

- care and maintenance
- SEO foundation plus reporting
- content growth
- conversion improvement
- technical support and enhancements

Each retainer should have:

- deliverables
- SLA expectations
- communication cadence
- exclusions
- upsell path

### 2. Build reporting systems

Use existing strengths like `monthly-report` and related business skills to define:

- monthly reporting format
- KPI set by client type
- narrative explanation model
- action recommendation structure

### 3. Automate internal repetitive work

Target automation for:

- project setup
- asset organization
- QA checks
- reporting preparation
- proposal assembly
- documentation generation

The goal is not automation for its own sake. It is margin expansion and consistency.

### 4. Formalize expansion offers

After a site is live, common next-step offers should be predefined:

- landing page expansion
- multilingual rollout
- blog and authority engine
- lead magnet funnel
- CRO sprint
- analytics refinement

### 5. Build client success discipline

Establish a client success rhythm:

- 30-day review
- 90-day review
- quarterly roadmap review
- renewal and expansion review

## Repository Outputs Required

- retainer package definitions
- monthly report templates
- post-launch review cadence documents
- automation opportunities backlog
- upsell and expansion matrix

## Success Criteria

- each completed website has a clear post-launch commercial path
- recurring revenue becomes part of the agency model
- repetitive work consumes less high-value founder time
- reports reinforce authority instead of feeling administrative
- client lifetime value grows without random upselling

## Risks to Avoid

- offering retainers with vague deliverables
- promising unlimited support
- automating client-facing work before the process itself is stable
- producing reports with no actionable interpretation
- chasing recurring revenue that low-quality clients do not value

## Reading Material To Buy And Provide At This Stage

- *Subscribed* by Tien Tzuo: recurring revenue logic and customer lifecycle thinking
- *The Automatic Customer* by John Warrillow: designing recurring offers
- *Agency Blueprint* by Paul Roetzer: agency systems, services, and operational maturity
- *Measure What Matters* by John Doerr: KPI discipline and outcome tracking
