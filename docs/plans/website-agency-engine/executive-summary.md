# Executive Summary - World-Class Agency Engine Plan

## Purpose

Bring the website-skills repository from its current state of "advanced agency engine"
(8.5/10 per the 2026-04-12 evaluation) to **9.5+/10** - a system that can credibly claim
to be the best web design practice in Africa and a serious player in the global premium
web design market.

## Current Position

Phases 1-9 of this roadmap have already closed the structural gaps identified in the
earliest assessment of the repository. The system today has:

- premium offer architecture and qualification logic
- a canonical strategy brief and research engine
- a strong design system with sector-specific tokens
- conversion, content, and SEO production discipline
- launch, rollback, QA, and post-launch operations
- proof, authority, and lead-generation infrastructure
- retainer and revenue-expansion logic
- governance, role-based onboarding, maintenance cadence, and quality metrics definitions

The 2026-04-12 evaluation rates the system at **8.5/10**, with the strongest dimensions
being **Coverage (9/10)** and **Strategy and Commercial System (8.8/10)**.

## What The Evaluation Says Is Still Missing

The evaluation identifies seven concrete world-class gaps:

1. **Enforcement** - standards exist socially but not mechanically. Lighthouse, accessibility,
   QA, and rollback rules are stated but not gated by automation.
2. **Visual QA** - the rendered output has no first-class review loop. Screenshot diffing,
   hierarchy checks, overflow checks, and AI-slop detection on real pages are absent.
3. **Performance enforcement** - budgets are framed but not enforced; no canonical perf gate
   command, no route-level budgets, no CI failure on regression.
4. **Accessibility enforcement** - accessibility is respected as a distributed expectation
   rather than a dedicated operating layer; no first-class a11y gate.
5. **Canonical CI** - QA matrix, Playwright starter, and rollback runbooks exist but are
   not wired into one canonical automated pipeline that every project inherits.
6. **Multi-operator proof** - Phase 9 added the right structures but they have not been
   proven by a real strategist, builder, SEO operator, and QA operator running them.
7. **Documentation drift** - reduced but not eliminated; older references still carry dated
   framing and overlap.

The plan adds two more pressures the evaluation does not name explicitly but that the
system cannot reach world-class without:

8. **Experimentation and CRO operations** - no structured A/B programme, no hypothesis
   logbook, no statistical literacy training for operators.
9. **Live observability loop** - monthly-report exists but is not yet wired to live RUM,
   GA4, or error-tracking data; the quality scorecard is defined but not observable.
10. **African positioning** - the system is excellent for African work but does not yet
    publish, benchmark, or certify in a way that establishes it as the African standard.

## Sequencing Rationale

**Phase 10 must run first** because every later phase depends on knowing whether what was
shipped is actually good. Without enforcement gates, "we ship premium quality" remains a
claim rather than a measurable property.

**Phase 11 follows** because once the gates exist, the engine needs the operating
discipline to keep them enforced as the team grows: certified operators, drift-free
documentation, live observability, a structured experimentation rhythm, and rendered-output
quality scoring.

**Phase 12 ships last** because public authority claims should rest on demonstrable,
measured excellence. A certification programme, open-source benchmarks, and a continental
voice are credible only after the gates are real and the operators are proven.

Run them in order. Skipping ahead to Phase 12 without Phase 10's gates produces the same
mistake the agency industry already makes - claiming world-class without proving it.

## What "World-Class" Means In This Plan

A world-class engine must satisfy six tests, all observable:

1. **Hard quality**: every project passes machine-enforced performance, accessibility,
   visual, security, and SEO gates before launch.
2. **Repeatable**: a second or third operator can run a project from intake to launch with
   the same quality variance as the founder.
3. **Measurable**: client outcomes (conversion, traffic, revenue impact) flow back into
   the repository's quality dashboard.
4. **Premium**: average project value remains above $12,000 with margin above 60% and
   refund/dispute rate below 2%.
5. **African-relevant**: the system works on 3G mobile, supports African languages and
   payment methods, respects local trust signals, and has been used to ship at least 12
   African client projects within 12 months of completing Phase 12.
6. **Publicly authoritative**: the agency is cited, referenced, or imitated by other
   African studios because its standards are public.

## Critical Risks To The Plan

- **Skipping enforcement** because gates feel slow or unfun. The evaluation is explicit
  that this is the primary remaining limiter.
- **Treating Phase 11 as optional** because the system "feels" governable. Multi-operator
  proof is the only valid evidence of governance.
- **Front-running Phase 12** with marketing before the underlying quality is enforced.
  This is how agencies destroy authority.
- **Letting documentation drift accelerate** as the repository grows. Phase 11 includes
  drift-eradication automation specifically to prevent this.
- **Forgetting Africa context** in Phases 10 and 11. Performance budgets, accessibility
  rules, and observability must reflect the constraints of the actual market the agency
  serves.

## Definition Of Done

The plan is complete when:

- Phases 10-12 all show "complete" in the table above.
- The 2026-04-12 evaluation can be re-run and scores 9.5/10 or higher.
- At least three operators (other than the founder) have run end-to-end projects through
  the system with a quality variance below 5% on the published scorecard.
- The repository's open-source benchmark sites are live and indexed.
- The certification programme has at least its first cohort enrolled.

## Bottom Line

The system has crossed from prompt library to advanced agency engine. The remaining work
is not about more thought; it is about installing the few enforcement and authority layers
that turn excellent thinking into excellent and observable practice. Phases 10-12 are how
that happens.
