## Trigger and baseline

Three supplied local Markdown books were requested for critical study:

- Rob Garner, *Search and Social: The Definitive Guide to Real-Time Content
  Marketing* (2012/13 context);
- Itamar Blauer and Andy Woolley, *Keywords for SEO: Actionable Knowledge Bombs
  to Help you Rank on Google in 2021*; and
- Wendy Bishop and David Starkey, *Keywords in Creative Writing* (2006).

Before this change, website skills had a strong AI-search currentness and
people-first baseline, but their book-informed guidance was concentrated on
AI-search response modes and entity clarity. The gap was an explicit bridge
from customer journey and intent research to distinctive editorial voice,
native distribution, and approval handoffs.

## Standardisation decision

Standardise the lens as qualified guidance. Do not standardise any book-specific
platform roster, interface instruction, social-signal/ranking claim, fixed
keyword rule, algorithm assertion, statistic, or analytics-product workflow.
Those remain historical, stale, or `NOT_ASSESSED` until a current primary source
supports the exact claim.

## Pilot and release gates

Pilot one real page or content cluster. Record the page job, audience, intent
map, source and proof map, voice notes, canonical route, rights/approval state,
failure path, baseline, guardrail, owner, and review date. Measure visibility or
citation observation separately from referral, qualified action, and revenue.

Release is blocked by fabricated proof, keyword stuffing, unsupported current
claims, a rights/privacy issue, inaccessible copy, or an unclear page job.
