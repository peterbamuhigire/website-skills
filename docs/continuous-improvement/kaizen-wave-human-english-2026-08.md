# Kaizen Wave — Human English for Website Content

Date: 2026-08-27
Scope: pages, landing pages, blogs, SEO/GEO assets, CTAs, forms, error messages, emails, and support messages.

## Baseline

This language-craft screening scored 58/100; it is not a visual, accessibility, SEO, security, or conversion certification. Strengths were reader-first content skills, native-language routing, premium commercial writing, and anti-slop quality gates. Gaps were the absence of a local synthesis of the supplied books and no single overlay connecting page job, genre, grammar, collocation, rhythm, state copy, and proof.

## Root cause

Website guidance had strong structure and conversion controls, but repeated page formulas can flatten voice. The engine needed a shared editorial method that treats a web page as a human reading experience rather than a keyword container.

## Improvement implemented

- Added `book-extractions/human-english-craft-synthesis-2026.md` with source limits, five passes, website register, grammar/lexical checks, state-copy guidance, and original anti-slop tests.
- Routed it through `AGENTS.md`, content-writing, premium-commercial-writing, and language-standards.
- Preserved SEO, accessibility, visual, security, performance, and evidence gates; a fluent page cannot certify an unsupported claim.

## Engine-level experiment and evidence

Experiment: verify that the local reference exists and is linked from the router and three anchor content/language gates.
Success criterion: links resolve without changing technical or accessibility controls.
Result: PASS — the local reference and three anchor content/language links resolve. Conversion or reader improvement remains unclaimed until rendered, accessible, human-reviewed samples are tested.

## Guardrails and rollback

If validation fails, remove only the new links. Do not introduce fake colloquialisms, errors, keyword stuffing, invented testimonials, or unsupported superlatives.

## Acceptance and next review

Review one home page, one service page, one blog article, one form/error flow, and one CTA set with two human editors plus visual/accessibility checks. Require page-job clarity, proof near claims, correct grammar, natural rhythm, useful headings, complete states, and no blocking slop finding. Re-audit date: 2026-09-26.
