# Hospitality, hotel and restaurant Kaizen - 2026-09-14

## Change

Added a hospitality website product route for hotel, resort, lodge, inn,
guest-house, restaurant, venue, catering and food-service websites. It joins
guest intent, staff reality, direct conversion, policy clarity, accessibility,
performance, truthful schema and post-click recovery.

The route also corrects the search-language layer: SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO and SXO
are useful lenses, but fixed answer length, training-data inclusion, AI recall,
ranking and third-party citation are not guaranteed outcomes. Facts, entities,
menus, rates, policies and reviews must be current and attributable.

## Evidence discipline

Hospitality books informed durable content/experience principles. Google Search
documentation, W3C accessibility guidance, OpenAI crawler documentation and
other primary sources govern current platform claims; the website search-AI
register remains authoritative. Schema is implemented only when the page and
consumer actually support it.

## Experiment and gate

Hypothesis: task-first hospitality IA with verified facts will improve qualified
booking/enquiry completion without reducing trust. Measure completion, errors,
response quality, accessibility defects, performance and referral attribution
on a real journey. Rollback the overlay link; do not claim ranking or conversion
lift without production evidence. Next review: 2026-10-14.
