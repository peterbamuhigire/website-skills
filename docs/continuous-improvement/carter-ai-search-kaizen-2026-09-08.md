## Experiment and standardisation

**Hypothesis:** an explicit concept-only book synthesis plus current-source
handoff will improve reuse of durable ideas while reducing platform folklore and
outcome conflation.

**Primary measure:** changed skills reference the synthesis and current source
register without unsupported universal thresholds or guarantees.

**Guardrails:** no raw-book reproduction, no unverified current claims, no
production mutation, no ranking/citation promise, and no weakening of existing
Google or permission gates.

**Standardised changes:** added the qualified synthesis; added the book to the
website router and SEO references; preserved current official guidance; and
made outcome separation, entity clarity, response-mode planning, and reversible
experiments explicit.
