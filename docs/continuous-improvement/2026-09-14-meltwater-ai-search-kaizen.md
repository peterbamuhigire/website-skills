## Evidence decision

The supplied DOCX is treated as a research brief, not as independent authority.
Its useful contribution is an experiment design: answer real buyer questions,
make content independently useful, use clear structure and named entities,
coordinate credible people with institutional pages, and separate citation
observation from referral, engagement, and conversion. The underlying vendor
study is limited to its named B2B prompt sample, categories, products, date,
and methodology. No universal LinkedIn preference or AI-training-data claim was
added.

## Quality gates

The source registers retain publication/access dates, tier, scope, uncertainty,
review date, and support status. Validation must include model-policy checks,
source freshness, skill-contract/routing checks, search-doctrine validation,
anti-slop review, and the engines' test suites. Raw DOCX text and OCR are not
committed.
