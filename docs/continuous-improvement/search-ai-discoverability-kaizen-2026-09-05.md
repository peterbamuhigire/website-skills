# Search and AI Discoverability Kaizen — 2026-09-05

## Outcome

The website engine now uses one layered, evidence-backed discoverability model:
technical SEO and useful content are the foundation; answer clarity and GEO are
overlapping retrieval/presentation practices; entity presence supplies a
verifiable public record; and SXO connects visibility to task success and
business outcomes.

This operation does not certify “world leader” status. It removes material
doctrine debt and establishes stronger currentness and regression controls. Live
site outcomes, platform accounts, user evidence, and independent review remain
`NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Aim and scope

- **Aim:** make websites built with this engine discoverable through
  conventional search and eligible, understandable, citable, accurately
  represented, and useful across AI-assisted discovery without folklore.
- **Scope:** `seo`, `seo-audit`, `google-ai-search`, their active references,
  routing, currentness evidence, engine manifest, CI, and tests.
- **Owner:** website-skills maintainer.
- **Currentness date:** 2026-09-05.
- **Re-audit:** 2026-12-05 or sooner after a provider changes crawler,
  structured-data, reporting, or inclusion controls.
- **Rollback:** revert this bounded change set or the future commit containing
  it; preserve the source register and failed evidence for review.

## Experiment and standardisation

**Hypothesis:** replacing scattered tactic lists with one layered doctrine and a
machine currentness gate will remove contradictory advice while preserving clean
routing to Google policy, cross-platform implementation, and read-only audit.

**Measure:** zero forbidden myths in canonical files, complete claim register,
100% routing fixtures, zero-debt skill contracts, passing tests, no source-
ingestion findings, and full canonical skill compliance.

**Guardrails:** preserve implementation/audit permission boundaries, do not
promise platform outcomes, do not alter production accounts or sites, and keep
missing live evidence visible.

**Standardised changes:**

- created the layered cross-platform doctrine and AI observation protocol;
- separated model training, automatic search retrieval, and user-triggered agents;
- added current Google generative AI reporting and inclusion controls;
- corrected Bing public-preview measures and interpretation limits;
- replaced deprecated sitemap ping automation with supported sitemap and
  IndexNow workflows;
- changed schema from mandatory catalogues to evidence- and consumer-matched use;
- removed rigid keyword density, word count, title length, CTR, traffic, bounce,
  conversion, payback, voice-search, and freshness rules from canonical guidance;
- rebuilt SEO writing, audit, E-E-A-T evidence, local, analytics, webmaster,
  post-deploy, and measurement references;
- added positive and collision routing fixtures;
- added `validate-search-doctrine.py`, CI enforcement, currentness expiry, and
  declared engine-manifest checks.

## Skill safety audit

The complete changed skill surface, linked references, new policy, validator,
tests, workflow, and engine manifest were inspected. The new validator is
read-only: it reads canonical Markdown, JSON policy, and the currentness register;
it performs no network access, process execution, credential access, file write,
deletion, installation, or publication. Search Console, Bing, crawler, profile,
and submission actions remain explicitly authority-gated in the skills.

**Verdict:** ACCEPT for the changed skill surface; no critical, high, medium, or
low safety finding remains. Live provider behaviour and account permissions stay
`NOT_ASSESSED` until a real project supplies authorised access.
