## Executive Summary

Of the 100 tweaks analysed, **37 are already well-covered** by our existing skills, **34 contain actionable enhancements** that would strengthen specific skills, **18 are partially covered but could benefit from explicit rules**, and **11 are not applicable** to our static-site context (analytics, A/B testing, email marketing tools).

The most impactful enhancements cluster around three areas:
1. **Visitor-centric copywriting rules** (page-builder, brand-alignment) — 15 tweaks
2. **Layout & visual hierarchy anti-patterns** (page-builder, design-system) — 12 tweaks
3. **Conversion architecture** (page-builder, brand-alignment) — 7 tweaks

## Recommended Enhancements by Skill

### 1. page-builder/SKILL.md (or page-builder/references/website-copywriting.md)
**20 enhancements — highest impact**

#### A. New Copy Rules to Add
1. **I/You Ratio Test** (Tips 14, 80, 98): "you"/"your" must outnumber "I"/"my"/"we"/"our" on every page. Never refer to visitors in third person. Use "we" only for working-together descriptions.
2. **Eugene Schwartz Awareness Levels** (Tip 7): Match copy depth to page awareness level — Contact page = most aware (show the deal), Homepage = problem-aware (show you understand), Service page = solution-aware (show how/why you're different).
3. **5 Sources of Uncertainty Checklist** (Tip 32): Every service/product page must address: (1) Unclear meaning, (2) Unknown pricing, (3) Unclear process, (4) No quality proof, (5) Can't gauge personality.
4. **BS Test** (Tip 54): "Would this copy create an awkward silence in real conversation?" Plus 5 tagline formulas.
5. **Questions-in-Copy Rule** (Tip 41): Only questions that get a "yes" from target audience. Communicate benefit in the question. Max 2-3 per section. Each question = friction.
6. **Google Translate Test** (Tip 56): Quality gate for detecting vague, overly clever copy.

#### B. New Layout/Design Rules to Add
7. **Reading Measure** (Tip 24): Body text width ~50-60% of desktop screen (max-w-prose or ~65ch). Never span full width.
8. **No False Bottoms** (Tip 68): Avoid section designs that look like page endings when content continues below.
9. **Bullet Point Spacing** (Tip 37): List items must have vertical spacing between them (space-y on `ul`/`ol`).
10. **60% Unhighlighted Rule** (Tip 57): On any screen view, at least 60% of text must be plain (not bold/highlighted). Max 3 consecutive one-line paragraphs (Tip 12).
11. **Social Icons Footer Only** (Tip 8): Social media links in footer only, never header/sidebar/body.
12. **No Body Links on Presentation Pages** (Tip 47): Homepage, About, and service pages = no inline text links. CTA buttons only.
13. **Complex Topic = Simpler Design** (Tip 86): Intellectually demanding content needs fewer visual elements, unified styling, shorter paragraphs.
14. **Hidden-Info Anti-Patterns** (Tip 89): No critical info on hover-only, behind tabs, via text links to other pages, or in carousels.

#### C. Enhanced Existing Rules
15. **No Carousels** (Tip 3): Make explicit ban on auto-rotating carousels for all contexts (testimonials, logos, heroes).
16. **CTA Placement** (Tip 11): Position CTAs after sufficient persuasive copy, not before.
17. **CTA Isolation** (Tip 73, 85): Max 1 CTA per screen view. Remove competing elements near primary CTA.
18. **Homepage ≠ Nav Mirror** (Tip 59): Homepage sections must provide context, not repeat nav labels.
19. **Service Page Completeness** (Tip 15): Must include process, what happens next, social proof, satisfaction policy, pricing.
20. **Text-on-Image Readability** (Tip 13): Checklist for hero images with text overlays.

## Key Insights from the Book That Align with Our Philosophy

Several of Andrews' core principles strongly validate our existing approach:

1. **"Every site must look different"** — She warns against copying competitors blindly (Tip 88) and emphasises niche-appropriate design (Tip 95), which matches our sector-strategies system.

2. **"Content from docs only"** — She repeatedly stresses knowing the product deeply before writing (Tips 29, 92), which matches our brand-alignment requirement.

3. **"Client Language Mining"** — She has an entire tip (97) dedicated to mining customer language from testimonials, which matches our existing copywriting reference.

4. **"Human voice mandatory"** — Her warnings against condescending copy (Tip 26), self-centered language (Tip 14), and vague/fake benefits (Tips 54, 62) align with our human voice standards.

5. **"Zero unnecessary JS"** — Her "don't add junk" philosophy (Tip 66) and anti-carousel stance (Tip 3) match our Astro zero-JS-by-default approach.
