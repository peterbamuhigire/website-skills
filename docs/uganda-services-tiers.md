# Uganda Website Services — Tiered Price Ranges

**Prepared:** 2026-06-03
**Conversion rate used:** 1 USD = UGX 3,800 (Bank of Uganda May 2026 indicators cluster around
UGX 3,700–3,800/USD; state the live bank selling rate and date in every real proposal).
**Grounding:** Quality standards, scope-control logic, payment design and no-go signals are taken
from the engine's `skills/agency-ops/agency-positioning/references/uganda-market-pricing-calibration.md`
and `pricing-rules.md`. **Price points are operator-set for the Ugandan SME market**; the high end now
overlaps the engine's premium Foundation band (see "Where this sits vs the premium engine" below).

> **How to read this.** Setup costs are **one-off**. Retainer and content costs are **per month**.
> Tiers reflect *scope and business maturity, not quality* — the engine's gates (accessibility, SEO,
> security, performance, content architecture) never drop between tiers. To meet a lower budget,
> narrow scope; never dilute the standard. (See "Scope control" at the foot of this document.)

### Where this sits vs the premium engine

The engine's premium Uganda ladder runs Foundation $1,500–$6,000 (≈UGX 5.7M–22.8M), Growth
$6,000–$18,000 (≈UGX 22.8M–68.4M), Authority $18,000+ (≈UGX 68.4M+). The tiers in **this** document
sit at the entry-to-Foundation end of that ladder: the **low end** is a productized SME entry package
below the premium floor, while the **high end** reaches into the premium Foundation band. Where a
buyer's stipulated value clearly supports it, promote them onto the full premium ladder rather than
stretching these packages.

| This document | One-off (UGX) | Buyer reality | Engine relationship |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Low end** | 2.5M – 5M | Micro / small SME | Below premium fit → productized entry package |
| **Mid range** | 6M – 12M | Established SME | Within the premium Foundation band |

## SET 1 — Website Designing (One-Time Setup Costs)

One-off project fee. Includes: web redesigning, functionality upgrade, security + SEO audit, and
social media integration. Charged as a fixed project fee, never per hour.

### Tier summary

| Tier | One-off bundle (UGX) | One-off bundle (USD) |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Low end** (bare acceptable minimum) | UGX 2.5M – 5M | $658 – $1,316 |
| **Mid range** | UGX 6M – 12M | $1,579 – $3,158 |
| **High end** | UGX 16M – 30M | $4,211 – $7,895 |

### Component breakdown (one-off)

| Component | Low end | Mid range | High end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1. Web redesigning | UGX 1.4M – 2.8M | UGX 3.5M – 7M | UGX 9M – 17M |
| 2. Functionality upgrade | UGX 0.6M – 1.2M | UGX 1.3M – 2.8M | UGX 4M – 8M |
| 3. Security + SEO audit | UGX 0.3M – 0.6M | UGX 0.7M – 1.4M | UGX 1.8M – 3.5M |
| 4. Social media integration | UGX 0.2M – 0.4M | UGX 0.5M – 0.8M | UGX 1.2M – 1.5M |

> Components can be sold à la carte. A standalone **Security + SEO audit** (UGX 300K–3.5M depending on
> tier) doubles as a paid-diagnosis offer and is creditable toward a later build.

### What each tier includes

**Low end** — 4–6 core pages; mobile-first responsive redesign on a clean template system; basic
on-page SEO and metadata; one analytics property; HTTPS, security headers and a baseline vulnerability
check; social profile links and share/Open Graph setup. Functionality limited to a contact form +
WhatsApp/click-to-call + map. One language (English).

**Mid range** — 7–12 pages with a proper content structure; semi-custom design on a strengthened
template; on-page SEO with schema and internal linking; lead-capture form(s) and basic conversion
paths; functional upgrades such as a blog/CMS, bookings, gallery, or a small catalogue; analytics with
goal tracking; social feed embeds and share tracking. Optional second language (e.g. Luganda).

**High end** — 12–25 pages, multi-service; custom design system; topical SEO (schema, internal
linking, event tracking) and AI-search basics; richer functionality (multi-step forms, mobile-money or
basic payments, member/portal-lite, multi-language toggle); CRM/analytics integration;
pixel/conversion tracking and a content-to-social workflow; accessibility and performance checked
against the Africa 3G profile. Often bilingual (English + French or Luganda).

### Typical clients per tier

- **Low end** — Sole proprietors and micro-businesses needing a credible, findable presence: a single
  salon, hardware shop, small clinic, consultant, boda/transport service, church or community project.
  The buyer wants "a proper website that looks legitimate and shows up when people search our name."
- **Mid range** — Established SMEs with several services or a steady customer base: a guesthouse, a
  private school, a SACCO, a dental or eye clinic, a B2B supplier, an NGO. The site is a real marketing
  tool and they want leads, bookings, or enquiries from it.
- **High end** — Growing, multi-service SMEs (and small institutions) that treat the site as their
  primary channel: a logistics or agribusiness firm, a clinic group, a college, a regional retailer, an
  early-stage app/SaaS. There are 2–3 decision-makers and they expect proof, conversion paths, and
  measurable results.

### Payment terms (one-off)

- Low end: 60% start, 30% design sign-off, 10% launch.
- Mid range: 50% start, 30% design sign-off, 20% launch.
- High end: 50% start, 25% design sign-off, 15% content freeze, 10% launch.
- 7–10% courtesy discount for 100% on signing. No outcome-contingent or net-60+ terms.

Charged **per month**, billed monthly upfront, minimum 3-month term. Annual prepayment earns a 10%
discount (engine rule). A retainer buys ongoing access and care — not a fixed pool of hours. New

off on annual terms).

### What each tier includes

**Low end** — Managed shared hosting + domain renewal; monthly core/CMS/plugin updates, backups and
uptime monitoring; SSL and security-header upkeep; basic patching; email/WhatsApp support
(next-business-day, business hours); a one-page monthly analytics snapshot (traffic, top pages, form
submissions). UX fixes handled reactively.

**Mid range** — Managed hosting with staging; updates and tested backups; proactive security
monitoring and patching; same-day support during business hours; quarterly performance tuning (Core
Web Vitals, image/asset budgets) plus light monthly UX fixes; a proper monthly report with goals,
conversions and recommendations; a named contact and a monthly check-in.

**High end** — Managed hosting with CDN, staging and rollback-ready deploys; weekly updates and
monitoring (uptime + error tracking); priority support with agreed response times; monthly performance
+ UX optimisation and small CRO tweaks; analytics dashboard and a strategic monthly report; named
account manager and roadmap planning.

### Typical clients per tier

- **Low end** — The low-end build client who just needs the site kept up, safe, current and backed up.
- **Mid range** — The mid-range client using the site as a working lead/booking channel and wanting it
  measured and steadily improved.
- **High end** — The growing multi-service SME that depends on the site for revenue and wants priority
  support, monitoring and someone accountable month to month.

## SET 3 — Content Generation + Engagement + Add-ons

Charged **per month**. Content builds most of the ongoing value (SEO, authority, leads, social
engagement). All content meets the engine's human-voice and language standards; multilingual
production (English / French / Swahili) is a premium-tier capability.

### Tier summary

| Tier | Content (UGX / month) | Content (USD / month) | Output level |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Basic** | UGX 350K – 800K | $92 – $211 | Keep-the-lights-on |
| **Standard** | UGX 1M – 2.5M | $263 – $658 | Consistent growth engine |
| **Premium** | UGX 3M – 7M | $789 – $1,842 | Authority + multilingual |

### What each tier includes (per month)

**Basic** — 1–2 SEO-aware blog posts or articles; 4–8 social posts (1–2 platforms) with simple branded
graphics; captions and hashtags; light comment monitoring. Single language (English). For businesses
that need a steady, credible drumbeat without a marketing function.

**Standard** — 3–4 optimised articles built around a keyword/topic plan; a managed social calendar
across 2–3 platforms (12–20 posts) with custom graphics; 1 light email/newsletter; a basic on-page SEO
refresh; monthly content performance notes; community engagement. Optional second language (Luganda or
French).

**Premium** — Full editorial system: 5–8+ long-form pieces / topical hubs; multilingual production
(English + French + Swahili) where the audience needs it; light photography and short-form video;
designed lead magnets and proof assets (case studies, guides); multi-platform social with ad-ready
creative; an email sequence; AI-search-optimised content; continuous content-led SEO with a quarterly
review. For growth-stage SMEs building category authority.

## Scope control, no-go signals, and fairness

**Scope control (when a client can't meet the band).** Reduce page count, prototype fidelity,
interview count, copy volume, or the optimisation window — never the research quality, accessibility,
analytics setup, or content architecture. Use staged milestones, not discounting.

**No-go signals (decline or re-scope).**

- Wants a high-end-looking site but refuses content, SEO, and QA.
- Asks for a discount while keeping scope unchanged (a discount = a scope reduction).
- Compares the work to a template-only vendor on price alone.
- Budget can't even support the low-end bundle and the result would be reputation-damaging.

**Promote to the premium ladder when** the buyer's stipulated value (revenue, leads, deal size,
institutional credibility) clearly supports a Growth ($6,000+ / UGX 22.8M+) or Authority engagement.
Don't stretch these packages to do an Authority job — move the buyer onto the premium ladder in
`uganda-market-pricing-calibration.md`.

**Pricing-conversation framing.** "These packages are calibrated for Ugandan SMEs around scope and
business stage. We don't remove the quality gates — we narrow the scope to what can be delivered
properly. If your goals are bigger, we move you to the right package rather than over-stretch this one."

**Quote discipline.** Always quote a fixed project fee (setup) or a monthly retainer/content fee —
never an hourly or daily rate. Convert to UGX at the live bank selling rate on the proposal date and
state the rate and date used.
