# Message Trust and Uncertainty Experiments

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sara Rubinelli,
*Institutional Health Communication in the Information Age*. It turns message
quality into a controlled website experiment; do not A/B-test unsafe medical
claims or treat engagement as proof of health impact.

## Experiment design

Define one audience, one decision, one evidence-backed message change, and one
guardrail. Test comprehension, correct next action, trust, accessibility, and
misunderstanding alongside reach or conversion. Record the source version,
message version, dates, audience exclusions, result, and decision.

## Stop conditions

Stop and review when a variant increases confusion, false certainty, stigma,
unsafe action, unequal access, or unverified claims. Preserve the safer baseline
and publish a correction when an error reaches users.
