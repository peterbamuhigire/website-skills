# Conversion Rules for Page Building

Parent skill: [page-builder](../SKILL.md). Read when building or reviewing a sales, landing, lead or order page. These are build defaults and hypotheses to test on the client's own traffic, not proven facts. No effect sizes are quoted: measure them yourself.

## Principles
- Test one element at a time, in this priority: offer, headline, CTA presence and timing, price and payment structure, layout.
- Fewer choices usually help. When unsure, remove an element before adding one.
- Warm traffic (an existing list) and cold traffic (ads) behave differently; build separate pages where budget allows.
- A plain page that converts beats a handsome one that does not. Never trade away accessibility or honesty for a test.

## Headlines
- Use the exact true figure ("1,148 shops") rather than a rounded one.
- Tie the claim to a specific real event or result you can evidence.
- Write in the present tense: what happens now, not what "will" happen.
- Lead with what the visitor gets and how soon, not what the product is.
- Test a high-contrast, warm headline colour against the brand colour, checking accessible contrast.
- On lead-magnet pages, test a curiosity headline that hints at the outcome without naming the offer.
- Keep human faces from sitting beside a headline; use a soft background image or a subject looking toward the copy.
- Test animated against static headlines; results differ by product.

## CTAs and Buttons
- Show the primary button immediately; never hide it until the price reveal.
- On video pages place it below the video; test above the video for engaged audiences.
- On long free-trial pages add a prominent button at the top.
- Make the primary CTA the single most dominant element (contrast, size). Only one element should look like that.
- Test button colours; start with the highest-contrast option against the page.
- Copy patterns: "Get access", "Claim your copy", "Start now", "Download free", "Try it free". Prefer action plus outcome to urgency alone.
- For text links: "You can [action] here".
- Add "free" or "no card needed" near a free-offer button only when true.

## Forms
- Lead-generation pages: test a fill-in-the-sentence form against a standard form.
- Ask one micro-commitment question before the email field; always include "Other".
- Offer email sign-up as well as any social login.
- Order forms: keep them inline on the sales page rather than redirecting.
- Use a two-step order form (contact details, then payment) and follow up, with consent, on contacts who stop at step two.
- Do not leave blank space on order forms; fill with short attributed testimonials.
- For offers of higher value add a two- or three-part payment plan and state the total cost; pre-select the option you want to anchor.
- Card-field layout: follow the payment provider's compliant components; test position only within them.

## Social Proof
- Place testimonials immediately after the claim they support, not in a separate block only.
- Put proof at the top of the order form above the offer recap.
- Stack video testimonials below the sales video under a proof heading.
- Test a large customer photo with the testimonial as headline copy on software and information pages.
- A short box beneath a sales video listing three to five points it covers may raise completion.
- A boxed list of six key highlights near the close, with a CTA inside, may beat a plain postscript.
- Honesty rule: show plain attributed quotes (name, role, photo), or a genuine social post natively with a link and consent. Never restyle a testimonial to imitate a post.
- Recent-purchase notices only if they reflect real, current activity.

## Layout and Visual Hierarchy
- Cut above-fold options to a handful; remove sections that do not advance the decision.
- On free webinar and lead-magnet pages, keep copy minimal.
- Place a visible CTA above the fold on long pages.
- Avoid a false bottom: alternate section backgrounds so the reader knows the page continues.
- Timed or scroll-triggered pop-ups: content pages only, never landing pages, never for existing subscribers, dismissible and accessible.
- Top notification bars on content pages: test for extra opt-ins without harming the main form.
- Mobile: do not assume responsive means better; test the phone experience by hand, and compare opt-ins and purchases separately. Consider desktop-only ad targeting until the mobile page is proven.

## Trust Signals
- Security seals on order and checkout pages only; test their absence on registration pages.
- HTTPS on every page.

## Pricing and Offers
- Express small discounts as currency amounts ("UGX 20,000 off"); test percentages for large prices.
- For cold social traffic test a payment plan as the lead option, for example three instalments against one payment.
- Keep one or two price options: a high anchor and a primary offer. Test removing the middle tier.
- For higher-value offers add a financing or instalment option, including in follow-up emails.
- Trial length: test different lengths.
- Trials: test no-card sign-up and compare sign-ups, paid conversions and support burden together.
- Consider selling the recurring product as the main offer with the entry item as a bonus; compare recurring accounts.

## Default Page Structures

**Lead page:** curiosity headline; optional three-step header (warm traffic only, final step labelled "Get your access"); micro-survey with four options ending in "Other"; email field; no navigation.

**Sales video page:** high-contrast headline; video with pause available; button visible from load; short points box; inline order form; proof section beneath.

**Order form:** attributed testimonials at the top; offer recap; card fields per provider; seals on the form only; short testimonials filling gaps; payment plan for higher-value offers.

## Trial-Close Copy
Short agreement questions placed through video, long page, webinar and email copy build "yes momentum" before the CTA. Place them after each testimonial, each benefit, before the price and before the CTA; on long pages at least once every three or four paragraphs. Slots: "Can you see how [outcome] would help your [business]?" / "Does that match what you need?" / "Would [result] be worth [price] to you?" Measure revenue per lead before and after; reduce frequency if the copy feels formulaic.

## Pre-Publication Checklist
- [ ] Headline uses a true, specific figure
- [ ] Headline contrast passes accessibility checks
- [ ] CTA copy is present tense and action-led
- [ ] Primary CTA visible immediately, below the video
- [ ] "Free" stated near CTA only if true
- [ ] Testimonials genuine, attributed and consented; any social format is a real linked post
- [ ] Testimonials at the top of the order form; no blank form space
- [ ] Points box under any sales video
- [ ] Order form inline; card fields follow provider guidance
- [ ] Seals on the order form only, not on registration pages
- [ ] At most two price options; discount unit tested
- [ ] Payment plan offered on higher-value offers, total cost stated
- [ ] Mobile tested by hand
- [ ] HTTPS on every page
- [ ] Free trial: card requirement decision tested and recorded
