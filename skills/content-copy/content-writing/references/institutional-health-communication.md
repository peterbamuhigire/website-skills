# Institutional Health Communication

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sara Rubinelli,
*Institutional Health Communication in the Information Age*. Use it for health
institution content planning and review; it does not authorise clinical advice or
replace qualified fact-checking.

## Content contract

Every message names its audience, purpose, evidence, action, uncertainty,
accessibility need, feedback route, and review owner. Translate technical
material into clear language while preserving limits, conditions, and routes to
professional care. Treat communication as dialogue: collect questions and
misunderstandings, then update the content when evidence or context changes.

## Trust checks

- Is the source and evidence role visible?
- Does the message distinguish established findings, emerging evidence, and opinion?
- Does it avoid false certainty, sensational conflict, and shame?
- Can affected or underserved audiences understand and act on it?
- Is there a correction path and a dated review trigger?
