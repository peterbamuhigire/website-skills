# English Output Overlay - Websites

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md) | Canonical study: `C:\wamp64\www\digital-research-skills\docs\continuous-improvement\english-collocations-and-lexical-precision-2026-09-02.md`

Apply after page intent, SEO, accessibility, and claim checks.

- Default to British English and a neutral East African register unless the target market says otherwise.
- Write for the visitor's question and next action. Prefer exact ordinary words, natural collocations, useful headings, and specific CTAs.
- Check a phrase in its grammatical frame and audience register. Do not force advanced words, idioms, metaphors, or local slang into copy for display.
- Keep idioms out of navigation, forms, errors, privacy, payments, technical explanations, and multilingual content unless a tested audience need justifies them.
- Replace praise with proof, process, scope, result, comparison, or honest qualification. Treat intensifiers as evidence-bearing claims.
- Proof copy in desktop/mobile context, including labels, buttons, error, loading, empty, success, privacy, and help states.
- Do not imitate humanity with typos, forced informality, generic hooks, repeated three-part lists, or fake warmth.

Record page job, reader, locale, source/proof map, terms and collocations checked,
idiom decision, accessibility review, proof status, gaps, reviewer, and date.

The supplied books are copyright-protected concept sources only; their lists and
examples are not a website copy bank. Current platform, SEO, accessibility, and
legal claims require Digital Research verification.
