# Niche Review Site Launch Checklist

Parent skill: [seo](../SKILL.md). Read when planning, building, or auditing a small content or review website that earns from affiliate links or referrals in a defined niche, or when choosing a niche and first keywords for such a site.

This checklist keeps the durable method (choose a rankable niche, publish useful reviews before promoting, build genuine relevance, diversify) and removes practices that current search-engine spam policies penalise. Check the current Google Search Essentials and spam policies before launch.

## 1. Choose the niche

1. Start from demand that already converts: products that sell in affiliate marketplaces and large retailers.
2. Look for rising interest with a trends tool, then find sub-niches inside the trend (accessories, guides, comparisons for a popular product rather than the product itself).
3. Prefer **medium niches**: large enough for active communities, small enough to avoid competing with major publishers.
4. Prefer niches where the builder has knowledge or genuine interest; the site needs sustained, first-hand content.

## 2. Score rankability

Use the keyword criteria in `seo-search/seo-audit/references/keyword-research.md` (volume, commercial intent, trend, weak competition). For this site type add:

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Local volume | Filter volume by the target country or region; global numbers mislead. Use exact-match volume. |
| Competition metric | Ignore advertising "competition" scores; they measure bid pressure, not ranking difficulty. |
| Page-level links | The top-ranking pages have few links pointing to the specific page. You compete with pages, not whole domains. |
| Page authority spread | Few first-page results are highly authoritative pages. |
| Top-three reach | You can plausibly reach the top three; clicks fall sharply below the first few positions and page two earns very little. Check current click-through data before quoting figures. |

Set numeric thresholds per project from current tool data; do not reuse historical thresholds as facts.

## 3. Domain and hosting

- Domain contains part of the core topic, is short, memorable, brandable, and free of hyphen strings and spammy keyword stacking.
- Use a trusted top-level domain appropriate to the audience.
- Use paid, reliable hosting, not free hosting or free subdomains.
- Treat multi-year domain registration as good practice for continuity, not as a ranking lever.

## 4. Content before promotion

Before any promotion:

- [ ] Publish several substantial, first-hand reviews or articles (set a minimum count and depth in the project brief).
- [ ] Schedule further articles for the coming weeks.
- [ ] Remove default or placeholder posts; apply a professional theme.
- [ ] Place affiliate links, with clear disclosure near the links and on a disclosure page.
- [ ] Target at least one article at the primary keyword.

Content types: product reviews, comparisons, tips, accessory and buying guides.

## 5. Content sourcing

| Method | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Write it yourself | Preferred. Study strong magazine cover lines and headline formulas for title ideas; adapt, do not copy. |
| Commission writers | Acceptable. Specify native-level language skill, verified reviews, clear standards, milestone or escrow payment, and editorial review. |
| Purchased reusable articles rewritten by software ("spinning") | Reject. Produces low-quality, scaled content that current spam policies target. |
| Unedited AI output at scale | Reject. Same risk; any AI assistance requires human expertise, fact-checking, and first-hand evidence. |

## 6. Images

Use only images with commercial-use rights: licensed stock, the builder's own photographs, public-domain sources, or openly licensed material with attribution as required. Record the licence for each image.

## 7. Monetisation

- Choose among digital-product affiliate marketplaces, retail affiliate programmes, or a combination. Check current commission rates and programme terms before planning revenue.
- Place links in relevant reviews; product widgets and relevant banners are secondary.
- Follow each programme's rules on link placement, pricing display, and disclosure.
- Expect a delay of at least several weeks before meaningful income; set expectations in writing.

## 8. Promotion and relevance

- Set alerts for the reviewed products, competitor products, and niche news.
- Join genuine discussions early on relevant blogs, forums, and communities. Contribute a considered, specific answer that adds value; mention the site only where it helps the reader and the community's rules allow it.
- Never post repetitive or copy-pasted comments, and never place comments mainly for links. Links in comments are typically marked as not passing ranking value; the benefit is referral traffic and reputation.
- One relevant, respected mention outweighs many generic links.
- Build links through useful resources, partnerships, and outreach consistent with current guidelines.

## 9. Scale and diversify

- Once revenue proves the model, delegate community participation to a trained assistant who reads and contributes genuinely; pay for quality and review the work.
- Add further sites in different niches to reduce dependence on one niche, programme, or algorithm update.
- Track traffic, clicks to merchants, conversions, and income per site monthly.

## 10. Commitment

Set a specific start date and first-month milestones before starting; the method only works when executed.
