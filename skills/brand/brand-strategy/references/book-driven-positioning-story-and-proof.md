# Book-driven positioning, story, and proof

This reference is an independent synthesis of durable principles from the supplied brand, archetype, storytelling, product-marketing, launch, market, and improvement books.

## Strategy before execution

Define the audience, job or tension, right to win, promise, proof, alternatives, boundaries, and desired behaviour before choosing pages, campaigns, or visual treatments. Record what the brand will not claim and which audiences are not being served.

## Human-centred narrative

Make the customer or stakeholder the active subject. Clarify desire, obstacle, stakes, decision, change, and evidence. Use an archetype only as a qualitative lens for voice and tension; it is a hypothesis to test, never audience proof.

## Proof and journey

Map each important promise to a proof asset, source, freshness, owner, and point in the journey. Align awareness, consideration, conversion, onboarding, use, support, retention, and advocacy. A launch brief should be the shared operating source for readiness, ownership, risk, feedback, and measurement.

## Kaizen loop

Baseline a user or business measure, run the smallest reversible experiment, inspect behaviour and outcomes, then standardise or revise. Keep qualitative evidence, quantitative evidence, and interpretation distinct. Accessibility, performance, trust, and content accuracy are product quality dimensions, not post-production decoration.

## Currentness gate

Current accessibility requirements, browser behaviour, platform capabilities, privacy or regulatory claims, security guidance, and technology versions must be verified through the Digital Research Engine. Use the current portfolio source register and the official source for the relevant claim; do not carry forward a book-era instruction merely because it is familiar.
