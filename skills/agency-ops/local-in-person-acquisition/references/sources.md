# Local In-Person Acquisition — Sources

**Purpose**: Attributed sources for this skill. Credit the practitioners and
research organisations whose work the scripts, cadences, and benchmarks encode.

**Read this when**: verifying a claim or citing a source in training.

## Confidence tiers

- **[P]** primary / named-organisation research.
- **[A]** stat-aggregator or practitioner blog — "industry-reported", confirm before stating as fact.
- **[B]** book / practitioner model — model reliable; confirm exact numbers against the text.

## Sales research (channel effectiveness, benchmarks)

- **[P] RAIN Group — Center for Sales Research.** 82% of buyers accept meetings
  with sellers who proactively reach out (study of 488 buyers / 489 sellers).
  https://www.rainsalestraining.com/sales-research/sales-prospecting-research
- **[P] HubSpot Research.** Ranks "meeting in person" as the #1 most effective
  sales channel. https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/sales-statistics
- **[A] SPOTIO — Door to Door Sales Guide.** 50–70 doors/day target; 30–40%
  contact rate; 30–40% close on qualified leads; track interactions not knocks;
  weekly KPI cadence. https://spotio.com/blog/door-to-door-sales/
- **[A] SalesRabbit.** Field-sales CRM; interaction-quality-over-volume framing.
  https://salesrabbit.com/free-door-knocking-app/

## Web-design-specific in-person selling

- **[A] SiteSwan.** The 30-second web-design pitch; the five SMB objections and
  rebuttal logic; local prospecting.
  https://www.siteswan.com/the-30-second-sales-pitch-for-web-designers-that-really-works
  · https://www.siteswan.com/5-common-sales-objections-web-designers-face-and-how-to-overcome-them
- **[A] QCFixer.** Web-design outreach: ~100 quality contacts → 2–5 clients; 80%
  of deals need ≥5 touchpoints; the Day 1/3/7/14 sequence; the 7-point quick

  is UNVERIFIED at primary).
  https://www.qcfixer.com/2026/04/19/how-to-approach-local-small-businesses-for-website-design-services/
- **[A] Selling Signals.** Lead-with-a-free-offer openers; walk-in / brick-and-mortar
  etiquette ("be a customer first"). https://sellingsignals.com/door-to-door-sales/
- **[A] Nutshell.** The "no budget" reframing. https://www.nutshell.com/blog/tips-beating-the-no-budget-objection

## Methods / books

  https://jebblount.com/product/fanatical-prospecting/

  Respond), cited via SPOTIO.
