# Delivery Automation — Sources

**Purpose**: Attributed sources for this skill. Credit the researchers and
practitioners whose evidence and models it encodes.

**Read this when**: verifying a claim or citing a source.

## Confidence tiers

- **[P]** primary / named-organisation research.
- **[A]** stat-aggregator / vendor blog — "industry-reported"; confirm before stating as fact.
- **[B]** book / practitioner model — model reliable; confirm exact numbers against the text.

## Primary research (the AI-reality anchor)

- **[P] METR — "Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced
  Open-Source Developer Productivity" (RCT, July 2025).** Experienced devs
  expected +24%, got −19% on real codebases.
  https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/ ·
  arXiv https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089
- **[P] Stack Overflow — 2025 Developer Survey.** 84% use/plan to use AI; trust
  in accuracy fell to ~29%; majority spend more time fixing "almost-right" code.
  https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai ·
  https://stackoverflow.co/company/press/archive/stack-overflow-2025-developer-survey/
- **[P] Faros AI — "The AI Productivity Paradox" (telemetry, ~10,000 devs /
  1,255 teams).** More PRs merged but PR review time +~91%, change-failure
  +~30%, no DORA improvement. https://www.faros.ai/ai-productivity-paradox

## Books / practitioner models

- **[B] John Warrillow — *Built to Sell* (Portfolio, 2011).** Teachable,
  Valuable, Repeatable; productize the one thing you do best at fixed scope/price.
  https://www.amazon.com/Built-Sell-Creating-Business-Without/dp/1591843979
- **[B] Michael Gerber — *The E-Myth*.** Work *on* the business via repeatable
  systems. (Referenced via systems-thinking literature.)

## UNVERIFIED — do not state as fact

- Margin figures ("40–75% productized vs 18–22% industry average"), valuation
  multiples ("2–4×"), the "McKinsey 2.7× hidden-cost" TCO claim, and "GitHub
  Copilot 55% faster" are second-hand attributions via blogs/summaries — confirm
  against primary reports before citing.
- Practitioner revenue figures (e.g. specific agency ARR/MRR) surfaced via vendor
  pages are unverified.
- METR, Stack Overflow 2025, and Faros are the solid quantitative anchors; prefer them.
