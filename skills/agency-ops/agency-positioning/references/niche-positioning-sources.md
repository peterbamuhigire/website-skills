# Niche Positioning — Sources

**Purpose**: Attributed source list for `references/niche-selection-rubric.md`.
Credit the practitioners and researchers whose models the rubric encodes.

**Read this when**: verifying a claim in the niche rubric, or citing a framework
in a proposal or training session.

## Primary frameworks (books / practitioners)

- **Blair Enns** — *The Win Without Pitching Manifesto* (2010). Proclamation #1,
  "We will specialize"; the three benefits of specialisation (win rate, price
  premium, relationship power). Founder, Win Without Pitching —
  https://winwithoutpitching.com
- **Philip Morgan** — *The Positioning Manual for Indie Consultants* / *for
  Technical Firms*. Five specialisation types; the 6-step selection process;
  Aggregate (Impact + Profit + Interest) and Risk (7 − Access − Credibility)
  scoring; the 1,000–10,000 prospect and 10–100 competitor guardrails (Morgan
  attributes the prospect range to David C. Baker's research) —
  https://philipmorgan.net/books-and-guides/specialization-guide/
- **David C. Baker** — *The Business of Expertise*; Punctuation (punctuation.com).
  "Default should be vertical"; vertical-vs-horizontal decision rules; T-shaped
  expertise; NAICS codes to define a vertical. *2Bobs* podcast, "How to Make
  Horizontal Positioning Work" — https://2bobs.com/podcast/how-to-make-horizontal-positioning-work
- **April Dunford** — *Obviously Awesome* (2019). Five components of positioning
  (competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value/proof, target-market
  characteristics, market category) —
  https://www.amazon.com/Obviously-Awesome-Product-Positioning-Customers/dp/1999023005
- **Seth Godin** — *This Is Marketing* (2018). Smallest Viable Market: "What's
  the smallest market you can survive on?" — https://www.shortform.com/blog/smallest-viable-market/

## Secondary analysis (agency blogs — confirm before quoting)

- Haus Advisors — vertical/horizontal framework and the "200+ named prospect"
  viability test: https://www.hausadvisors.com/blog/vertical-vs-horizontal-positioning
  and micro-niche math: https://www.hausadvisors.com/blog/agency-micro-niche-strategy
- Epom — vertical-agency expansion pattern (master one, expand to adjacent,
  reuse assets): https://epom.com/blog/digital-advertising/ad-vertical-strategy-for-ad-agencies
- Tim Kilroy — horizontal vs vertical growth: https://timkilroy.com/blog/horizontal-vs-vertical-growth/

## UNVERIFIED — do not state as fact

- Margin/pricing claims ("specialist agencies 25–40% margin vs generalist
  15–20%", "niche agencies charge 2–4× more") are vendor-blog assertions with no
  named primary study. Frame as "industry-reported" if used at all.
- The "≈15–20 clients before expansion" threshold is a blog assertion, not
  sourced research.
- Morgan attributes the 1,000–10,000 prospect range to David C. Baker; Baker's
  primary statement of that exact range was not independently located. Cite it
  as "Morgan, after Baker" rather than as Baker's own published number.
