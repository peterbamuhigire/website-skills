# Uganda Market Pricing Calibration

This reference is self-contained. It adapts the premium website and UI/UX pricing model
to Ugandan and East African buyers without importing US/UK agency fee floors as a rigid
local rule.

Sources used:

- https://www.designstudiouiux.com/blog/saas-ux-design-cost/
- https://www.designstudiouiux.com/blog/enterprise-website-design/
- Bank of Uganda May 2026 macro indicators for USD/UGX calibration.

## Core Rule

Local market calibration changes scope, payment design, and tier thresholds. It does not
change the quality standard for accepted work.

## Buyer Segments

| Buyer | Typical Budget Reality | How To Sell |
|---|---:|---|
| Micro or informal business | Below premium fit | Offer audit, template referral, or decline. Do not dilute the engine. |
| Local SME | $1,500-$6,000 | Sell a narrow Foundation system: strategy, core pages, UX, SEO basics, analytics, launch. |
| Established Ugandan company | $6,000-$18,000 | Sell Growth scope with discovery, proof, conversion paths, and stronger handoff. |
| Regulated, donor, NGO, school, hospital, enterprise | $12,000-$35,000 | Sell authority, compliance, stakeholder, accessibility, and governance value. |
| Export, regional, funded, or international buyer | $25,000-$85,000+ | Use global premium positioning and value-based pricing. |

Convert to UGX in proposals using the current Bank of Uganda or bank selling rate. Public
May 2026 USD/UGX sources cluster around UGX 3,700-3,800 per USD; state the rate and date
used in any proposal.

## Uganda-Calibrated Offer Ladder

| Tier | Local Floor | Typical Local Band | Use When |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Paid diagnosis / website audit | $300 | $300-$1,500 | Brief is unclear, buyer is not ready for build, or risk needs evidence. |
| Foundation Website System | $1,500 | $1,500-$6,000 | 5-10 core pages, trust rebuild, service business, basic SEO and analytics. |
| Growth Website System | $6,000 | $6,000-$18,000 | Multiple services, proof system, content architecture, CRM/analytics, lead generation. |
| Authority Website System | $18,000 | $18,000-$45,000 | Institution, regulated trust, donor/board scrutiny, category repositioning, large content system. |
| Regional / international authority | $35,000 | $35,000-$85,000+ | Export buyer, funded SaaS, multi-region, deep SEO/content/proof system. |

## Scope Control

If the client cannot meet the band:

- Reduce page count, not research quality.
- Reduce prototype fidelity, not accessibility.
- Reduce number of stakeholder interviews, not discovery.
- Reduce copy production, not content architecture.
- Reduce post-launch optimisation window, not analytics setup.
- Use staged milestones, not discounting.

## Payment Design

Ugandan buyers often need cash-flow-friendly terms. Accept this only if scope remains
protected.

| Engagement | Suggested Terms |
|---|---|
| Audit / diagnosis | 100% upfront |
| Foundation | 60% start, 30% design sign-off, 10% launch |
| Growth | 50% start, 25% design sign-off, 15% content freeze, 10% launch |
| Authority | 40% start, 25% strategy sign-off, 20% design/content sign-off, 15% launch |
| Retainer | Monthly upfront, minimum 3 months |

## Pricing Conversation

Use this framing:

The international benchmark for this kind of UX and website system is much higher, but
for Ugandan companies the package is calibrated around scope and business maturity. Do
not remove the quality gates; narrow the scope to the level that can be delivered
properly.

## No-Go Signals

- The buyer wants a premium-looking site but refuses discovery, content, proof, SEO, and QA.
- The buyer asks for a discount while keeping scope unchanged.
- The buyer compares the work to a template-only vendor.
- The budget is below the audit floor and the work would become reputation-damaging.
