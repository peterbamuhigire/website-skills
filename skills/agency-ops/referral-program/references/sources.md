# Referral Program — Sources

**Purpose**: Attributed sources for this skill. Credit the researchers and
practitioners whose evidence and models it encodes.

**Read this when**: verifying a claim or citing a source.

## Confidence tiers

- **[P]** peer-reviewed / named-organisation primary research.
- **[A]** stat-aggregator / vendor blog — "industry-reported"; confirm before stating as fact.
- **[B]** book / practitioner model — model reliable; confirm exact numbers against the text.

## Primary research (the anchor evidence)

- **[P] Schmitt, Skiera & Van den Bulte — "Referral Programs and Customer
  Value," *Journal of Marketing* 75(1), 2011.** Referred customers ~16% more
  valuable and ~18% less likely to churn; ~10,000 customers of a German bank
  tracked ~33 months; 2011 MSI/H. Paul Root Award.
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1509/jm.75.1.46 ·
  open PDF: https://www.marketing.uni-frankfurt.de/fileadmin/user_upload/dateien_abteilungen/abt_marketing/Bilder/Professor_Skiera/Publikationen/Do_Referral_Programs_Increase_Profits.pdf
- **[P] Van den Bulte, Bayer, Skiera & Schmitt — "How Customer Referral Programs
  Turn Social Capital into Economic Capital," *Journal of Marketing Research*,
  2018.** https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1509/jmr.14.0653

## Books / practitioner models

- **[B] John Jantsch — *The Referral Engine* (Portfolio, 2010).** Reframe the ask
  as helping them get more of what they want; set the referral expectation at
  kickoff; the referral toolbox; Know→Like→Trust→Try→Buy→Repeat→Refer.
  https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7717835-the-referral-engine
- **[B] Joey Coleman — *Never Lose a Customer Again* (Portfolio, 2018).** Eight
  phases ending in Advocate; 20–70% of new customers churn in the first 100 days.
  https://joeycoleman.com/books/never-lose-a-customer-again/
- **[B] Fred Reichheld & Rob Markey — *The Ultimate Question 2.0* (HBR Press,
  2011).** NPS; ask Promoters (9–10); Promoters generate most positive word of
  mouth. https://store.hbr.org/product/the-ultimate-question-2-0-revised-and-expanded-edition/10618

## UNVERIFIED — do not state as fact

- The Nielsen "92% trust recommendations / 4× more likely to buy" figures and the
  B2B "11% conversion / 71% higher / 69% faster / 84% start with a referral"
  cluster are aggregator-cited without a named primary study located here.
- All benchmark percentages (2.3% global referral rate, 12–15% participation, 78%
  double-sided) are vendor benchmarks, not peer-reviewed.
- Exact stats quoted from the books (Reichheld, Jantsch, Coleman) came via
  summaries; confirm against the texts before quoting numbers.
