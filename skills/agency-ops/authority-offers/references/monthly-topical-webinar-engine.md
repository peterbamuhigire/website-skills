# Monthly Topical Webinar Engine

Parent skill: [authority-offers](../SKILL.md). Read when setting up a recurring
webinar as the agency's main inbound source, planning a year of topics,
writing the registration and follow-up flow, or judging whether a webinar
worked.

`offer-ladder.md` places webinars on the ladder. This file runs them as a
monthly system. Live-event production and social promotion belong to the
social-media engine (social-media-skills); this file owns the offer, the
registration pages, the booking step and the measurement.

## 1. The core insight

Most registrants do not attend, and the busiest, best-fit prospects are the
least likely to attend while the most likely to register. So the value is in
**promoting** the webinar and in what the registration flow does, not only in
the live hour.

## 2. Topic bank with annual re-dating

1. Build 10–12 evergreen topics for the niche. Examples for a website agency:
   the annual website plan, getting found locally, turning visitors into
   enquiries, reviews and proof, speed on low-cost phones, what to measure.
2. Put the year in the title ("The [Year] [Niche] Website Plan"). Buyers want
   what works now.
3. Refresh and re-date each topic every year, updating every figure and
   example.
4. Schedule the date first, then prepare. A fixed date forces completion.

## 3. Registration flow with a booking step

1. **Registration page:** topic, who it is for, three things they will learn,
   date, time zone, form.
2. **Thank-you page:** a short video from the presenter: "Most [niche] owners
   want to understand this but would rather have an expert do it. Book a time
   and I'll show you how we'd do it for you." Booking link below.
3. **Confirmation message:** repeats the booking invitation.
4. **Optional pre-assessment:** a short form about their current site, which
   qualifies and prepares the booked call.

## 4. Three emails per webinar

Each webinar gives three legitimate, useful reasons to write to the list:

| Email | Timing | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation | 7–10 days before | Topic, what they will learn, registration link |
| Reminder | Day before or morning of | Time, link, one teaser point |
| Replay | Within 48 hours after | Recording, key points, booking link |

Send only to people who have consented to receive your email. Every message
identifies the sender and includes a working opt-out; honour objections (see
registers PL-01 and PL-02). Platform features, limits and deliverability rules
change; check the current terms of your webinar and email platforms before
each quarter.

## 5. Deliver and repurpose

- End the session with one call to action: book a review or download the
  niche checklist (see `diagnostic-entry-offers.md`).
- Repurpose one session into several assets: the full recording, the slides,
  short clips per key point, an audio episode, a transcript turned into an
  article. Confirm each platform's current format rules before publishing.

## 6. Follow up every registrant

- Contact every registrant within the week if capacity allows, starting with
  best-fit organisations. Offer the replay and a short review call; no pitch.
- **Count leads for 30 days after each webinar.** Late registrants and replay
  viewers keep booking. One practitioner reports webinars producing leads for
  about a month each; treat that as an anecdote and measure your own tail.

## 7. Measurement

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Registrations | Count, and share from best-fit organisations |
| Attendance | Live attendees ÷ registrations (reported, not optimised for) |
| Pre-assessments | Forms completed ÷ registrations |
| Bookings | Review calls booked within 30 days ÷ registrations |
| Wins | Core-programme clients won within six months from webinar-sourced leads |
| Cost per booking | Promotion and production cost ÷ bookings |

Nelson reports one run of 193 registrants, 58 pre-assessments and 19 strategy
sessions. That is **one practitioner's anecdote from a US agency**, useful only
to show the shape of the funnel. Set baselines from your own first three
months.

## 8. Monthly checklist

- [ ] Topic chosen from the bank and re-dated; date in the calendar.
- [ ] Registration page, thank-you video and booking link live and tested.
- [ ] Three emails scheduled to consented contacts only.
- [ ] Promotion brief handed to the social or media owner.
- [ ] Session delivered with one call to action.
- [ ] Replay sent; assets repurposed.
- [ ] Registrants contacted; 30-day lead count recorded.
