# Website value and delivery proof in an AI market

Parent: [Premium Website Product](../SKILL.md).

## Benchmark first

[Instrument describes](https://www.instrument.com/about) integrated strategy, design and technology teams and connected execution. [Apple describes](https://www.apple.com/accessibility/) accessibility as integral to its products. Accessed 2026-09-22. These are primary descriptions of their approach, not independent proof of results or permission to imitate their visual identities. Our synthesis: a premium website engagement must unite business judgement, authored design, implementation quality and continuing service.

## Decide whether bespoke work earns its price

Compare the credible alternatives before recommending a build:

| Alternative | Investigate | Bespoke work must justify |
|---|---|---|
| Improve the existing site | Existing equity, defects, ownership and migration risk | Why replacement beats a focused repair |
| Template or AI-assisted builder | Fit, content workload, exports, recurring costs and limits | Material unmet buyer tasks, proof, integration or service needs |
| Bespoke delivery | Research, content, design, implementation, QA and support | The additional value and responsibility purchased |
| Defer and fix the offer/service | Demand evidence, fulfilment, sales response and capacity | Why traffic investment is timely |

Use an alternatives workpaper: buyer job → present failure → proposed improvement → evidence → full lifecycle cost → owner → acceptance → uncertainty. Include agency/client labour, hosting, tools, licences, maintenance, migration and exit costs. Do not invent a minimum premium fee, conversion uplift or payback period. When evidence is weak, price a bounded discovery or pilot and state the decision it will unlock.

## Make the invisible work inspectable

1. **Discovery:** capture customer tasks, objections, buying process, content sources, analytics limits and operating constraints. Retain contradictions; do not replace interviews with AI personas.
2. **Architecture and content:** map each page to a user question, evidence, next action and maintenance owner. Identify what should not be built. Test service comprehension before polishing.
3. **Authored design:** route through design-system-skills. Record the visual thesis, distinctive decisions, approved references and asset rights. Review real content in context; copying Apple-like whitespace or a luxury palette is not an authored concept.
4. **Implementation:** define integrations, data flow, error paths, access controls, editing needs and performance budgets. Route custom application/backend work to the engineering and SRS engines.
5. **Acceptance:** test complete customer tasks, including failure and recovery. Keep source, build version, environment, evidence and unresolved defects together.
6. **Operation:** transfer ownership, train the client, rehearse recovery and define post-launch measurement, maintenance and escalation.

## Acceptance pack

| Dimension | Required evidence | False positive to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial comprehension | Representative users can explain the offer, fit, proof and next step | Attractive hero screenshot |
| Enquiry/booking | Submission reaches the right authorised recipient/CRM; confirmation and failure recovery work | Success toast with no received enquiry |
| Accessibility | Applicable WCAG criteria, keyboard/manual checks, assistive-technology evidence and defect retests | Automated score, badge or overlay alone |
| Performance | Lab diagnosis plus field data when available; device/network/page scope | Desktop Lighthouse score presented as universal experience |
| Search | Crawl/indexability checks, content intent, accurate metadata and supported structured data | Ranking or AI-citation guarantee |
| Security/privacy | Applicable controls, tested forms, authorised data flow, retention/consent behaviour | Policy text or consent banner alone |
| Maintainability | Client completes representative edit and safe recovery tasks | CMS login handed over without training |
| Ownership/exit | Domain, hosting, repository, content, analytics and licences mapped; access and export checked | Undisclosed lock-in |
| Service | Support hours, response vs resolution distinction, escalation, responsibilities and recovery plan | Undefined “lifetime support” |

[WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) supplies testable accessibility criteria; scope conformance to the actual pages and complete processes. [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) distinguishes field and lab measurement. Both opened 2026-09-22; recheck current guidance before delivery. A new site with no field population reports field performance as NOT_ASSESSED, while retaining its lab evidence and collection plan.

## Low-bandwidth and local fit

Agree representative phones, connections, languages, user abilities and payment/contact habits from evidence. Test image failure, slow connections, keyboard access, reduced motion and recoverable form errors. Keep WhatsApp, telephone and email as choices where they fit the audience; do not assume every buyer wants the same contact route. Verify jurisdiction-specific privacy, sector and commerce obligations independently.

## Demonstrate value after launch

Define the pre-launch baseline, observation window and measures before launch. Separate enquiries from qualified opportunities, wins, collected revenue and contribution. Explain tracking gaps, seasonality and other changes; before/after movement alone does not prove the redesign caused growth. For low-traffic sites, combine task research and operational evidence with commercial observations rather than promising a statistically powered A/B test.

At each review, show what was improved, what remains uncertain, which defects matter, and what investment should follow. Publish a case study only with permission and substantiated scope, baseline, intervention, period and limitations. An educational demonstration must never become a client testimonial.

## Synthetic acceptance challenge

A visually polished site loads quickly but its enquiry email is discarded and the client cannot export content. Release fails. Repair delivery monitoring and export/handover, retest both tasks, then reassess. Passing visual review cannot compensate for these defects.
