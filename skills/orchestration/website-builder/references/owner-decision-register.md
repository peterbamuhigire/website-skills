# Owner Decision Register

Parent skill: [website-builder](../SKILL.md). Read during discovery and at every
stage gate where a decision belongs to the client, not the agency.

Some website decisions only the owner can make. The agency explains the
options, recommends one and records the consequence; the owner decides. A
written register stops "we never agreed that" disputes, keeps scope honest and
shows the client that the agency respects their authority. It is separate from
the agency's own technical decision log in `project-log/decisions/`.

## Register format

| # | Decision | Options explained | Agency recommendation and reason | Owner decision | Decided by (name, role) | Date | Consequence or follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Minimum decisions

1. Site purpose: outcomes and jobs (from the intake "Why This Website" table).
2. Primary domain and defensive variants.
3. Registrant identity (must be the client).
4. Registrar.
5. Hosting type and region.
6. Email arrangement.
7. Build route: custom design, adapted template, hosted platform.
8. Content editing workflow (who edits, with what tool).
9. Style direction (from design-reference style-fit questions).
10. Page list and single-focus service pages.
11. Home-page priorities: at most two primary actions.
12. Blog: yes or no, and a cadence the client can sustain.
13. Selling route: none, enquiry, booking, hosted checkout, full cart.
14. Payment gateway and who owns the merchant account.
15. Newsletter or WhatsApp broadcast: yes or no, cadence, consent method.
16. Social platforms to link (by audience, not by fashion).
17. Paid media budget and who manages it (route strategy to the marketing engine).
18. Backup responsibility (agency care plan or client).
19. Maintenance responsibility and care-plan tier.
20. Who may change content, and approval rules.
21. Copyright notice and content-use licence.
22. Personal data collected, where it is stored, and the lawful basis.

Add project-specific rows (languages, accessibility target, integrations).

## Rules

- Present each decision in plain language with the cost, risk and time effect
  of each option.
- Record "not decided" with a due date rather than leaving a row blank.
- A decision that changes scope, price or timeline triggers a change request.
- Re-open a decision only with a new row that references the old one.

## Sources

- Plumley, G. (2011) *Website Design and Development: 100 Questions to Ask
  Before Building a Website*, Wiley Publishing — the owner-side decision points
  this register collects.
