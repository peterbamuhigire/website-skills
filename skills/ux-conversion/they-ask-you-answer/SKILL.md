# They Ask You Answer
Acknowledgement: Shared by Peter Bamuhigire, techguypeter.com, +256 784 464178.

<!-- dual-compat-start -->
## Use when
- A premium agency needs buyer questions turned into an authority library, editorial priorities, video plan, and assignment-selling workflow.
- Sales and content teams need a shared evidence register connecting Big 5/Selling 7 assets to real objections and buying stages.

## Do not use when
- The request is one article, landing page, or sales document without system planning; use the relevant writing skill.
- The request is social channel execution; use `social-media` after the authority plan is approved.
- No buyer-question or sales evidence exists; run research rather than inventing a calendar.

## Required Inputs
| Input | Source | Required | If absent |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Ideal buyers, offer, sales process, objections, and qualification criteria | Discovery and sales evidence | yes | Stop the editorial plan and request buyer/sales inputs. |
| Buyer questions from calls, search, email, support, and proposals | First-party/research sources | yes | Produce a research plan, not fabricated questions. |
| Claims, case evidence, pricing posture, owners, and publishing capacity | Client/agency records | yes | Exclude unsupported claims and reduce cadence to feasible scope. |

## Workflow
1. Read the current business context and the concrete task to solve.
2. Stop editorial planning when buyer-question evidence, claim proof, ownership, or publishing capacity is missing.
3. Use only the relevant detailed guidance and references for the request at hand.
3. Use `premium-commercial-writing` when buyer-question content must become premium website copy, SEO/GEO pages, articles, sales documents, or offer-page sections.
4. Prioritise questions by buyer frequency, sales friction, strategic fit, evidence, and feasible publishing capacity.
5. Decide format, proof, CTA, assignment-selling use, owner, cadence, and measurement for each selected item.
6. Validate claims, confidentiality, native-language needs, SEO intent, and sales adoption before handoff.
7. Recovery: if proof or capacity fails, reduce scope, preserve the research register, and rerun prioritisation after evidence or ownership is restored.

## Quality standards
- Every priority question traces to buyer or sales evidence and an intended sales/content use.
- Claims, examples, prices, results, and case material are verified, consented, or excluded.
- Cadence reflects named owners and sustainable production capacity.

## Anti-patterns
- Inventing buyer questions. Fix: trace them to calls, search, email, support, or proposals.
- Publishing claims without proof. Fix: verify, qualify, or remove them.
- Producing content with no sales use. Fix: assign a buying stage, owner, and touchpoint.
- Padding an infeasible calendar. Fix: reduce cadence to named capacity.
- Exposing confidential client material. Fix: obtain approval, anonymise safely, or exclude it.
- Do not produce generic framework dumps with no decision made.
- Do not invent facts to complete the output.
- Do not ignore the actual audience, offer, or business constraints.
- Do not invent buyer quotations, results, prices, case studies, or expertise.
- Do not publish transparency content that breaches confidentiality, consent, or client agreements.

## Outputs
| Artefact | Consumer | Observable acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer-question and content-priority register | Sales, content, SEO, and leadership | Every item traces to evidence, maps to a buying stage/use, and has an owner. |
| Editorial and assignment-selling plan | Writers and sales team | Names format, brief, proof, CTA, sales use, channel, cadence, and measurement for each item. |

## Evidence Produced
| Evidence | Format | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Question provenance register | Source, date/range, wording/theme, frequency, confidence | Distinguishes direct buyer language from editorial inference. |
| Content-use review | Asset, sales touchpoint, usage, outcome, revision | Does not claim pipeline impact without attributable evidence. |

## Capability Contract
Research and planning default to read-only access to approved sales/content evidence. Drafting is allowed when requested; publishing, contacting buyers, using confidential material, changing CRM records, or spending requires explicit authority and consent.

## Degraded Mode
When interviews, call notes, search evidence, case proof, or publishing capacity is unavailable, return a qualified research plan and mark inferred questions not assessed as buyer truth.

## Decision Rules
| Choice | Action | Failure or risk avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated high-friction buyer question | Prioritise a direct evidence-backed answer | Editorial irrelevance |
| Answer needs confidential or unsupported proof | Reframe, anonymise with approval, or stop | Breach or fabrication |
| Asset can shorten a named sales step | Add assignment use and owner | Content disconnected from sales |
| Capacity cannot sustain cadence | Reduce frequency and protect quality | Content churn |

## Worked Example
If proposal calls repeatedly ask why a premium site costs more, trace the question to call notes, build a comparison article with verified scope and trade-offs, assign it before discovery follow-up, and measure qualified follow-through. Do not invent a price range or promise close-rate improvement.

## Continental publishing rhythm (Phase 12 integration)

The editorial calendar integrates with Phase 12's continental-presence
workstream:

- **Quarterly cornerstone authority piece.** Africa-specific topic
  (low-bandwidth performance, mobile-money UX, multilingual design at
  scale, African trust patterns). Published on the agency site and
  offered to one African design/developer media outlet (TechCabal,
  TechPoint Africa, WeeTracker, Disrupt Africa, Smashing Magazine
  African contributors).
- **Quarterly open data or benchmark release.** Tied to the public
  scorecard regeneration; each release names a concrete metric,
  pattern, or finding.
- **Quarterly community guest contribution.** One piece from an
  invited African contributor per quarter; reciprocates the
  authority-sharing posture.
- **Conference talk or hosted workshop.** At least one per year at
  DevFest Africa, DroidConKE, PyCon Africa, ForLoop Africa, Africa
  Tech Summit, or a sector conference.
- **Media relationships.** Relationships maintained with at least
  three African design or developer media outlets.

Publishing cadence feeds and is fed by the quarterly public scorecard
regeneration and the cross-client experimentation review.
