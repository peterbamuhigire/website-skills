# Security Disclosure Policy

Use this page as the public vulnerability disclosure policy linked from
`/.well-known/security.txt`. Replace placeholders before launch.

## Reporting Security Issues

If you believe you have found a security issue affecting `{company name}` or
`{domain}`, report it to `{security contact email}`.

Please include:

- The affected URL, page, or system.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- The likely impact.
- Any screenshots, logs, or proof of concept that help us verify safely.

## Scope

In scope:

- Public website pages and forms under `https://{domain}/`.
- Exposed configuration, headers, or client-side assets shipped with the site.
- Data handling issues affecting contact forms, analytics consent, or policy pages.

Out of scope unless separately agreed:

- Denial-of-service testing.
- Social engineering.
- Physical attacks.
- Automated high-volume scanning that affects availability.
- Third-party platforms not controlled by `{company name}`.

## Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action for good-faith security research that stays
within this policy, avoids privacy harm, avoids disruption, and gives us a
reasonable opportunity to fix the issue before public disclosure.

## Response Targets

- Acknowledgement: within 3 business days.
- Initial triage: within 10 business days.
- Fix timeline: based on severity and business impact.

## Disclosure

Do not publicly disclose the issue until we have confirmed the fix or agreed a
disclosure date with you.
