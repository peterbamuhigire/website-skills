## Book-derived capabilities added

- Outside-in alignment, journey, experience, ecosystem, and mental-model mapping with fit-for-purpose selection.
- Premium discovery interview protocol with sample design, behaviour-led question bank, and validation thresholds.
- Translation of future-state experience maps into sitemap, page archetypes, CTA ladder, proof placement, form depth, and analytics events.
- Alignment workshop runbook with decision discipline.
- Service blueprint for agency delivery across discovery, strategy, design, build, launch, and retainer.
- Promise-to-operations traceability that audits website claims against operational capacity.
- Fail-point and recovery playbook with detection, SLAs, escalation matrix, and postmortem rules.
- Retainer archetypes, hours model, scope rules, change-request gate, profitability gate.
- Premium qualification and fit scoring (10–12 pursue, 7–9 paid discovery, 4–6 referral, 0–3 hard no).
- Premium objection handling with acknowledge → reframe → evidence → confirm pattern across price, timeline, scope, technology, trust, regional context, and stalls.
- Premium pricing defence: anchors, outcome pricing logic, discount discipline, floor-price violations.
- No-fit and referral templates that protect the agency's standard.
- Service language patterns (acknowledgement, holding, resolution, recovery, escalation, refund, exit) that replace generic apologies.
- Triage and escalation matrix with severity definitions, SLAs, on-call rota, and handoff discipline.
- Recovery and retention gestures with proportional response and tracking; explicit anti-pattern guidance against the apology economy.
- Post-launch hypercare, stabilisation, evidence, and renewal positioning ritual for the first 90 days.
- KPI tree pattern (business → behavioural → leading) with definition cards, target-setting rules, and worked examples for lead-gen, ecommerce, and content authority sites.
- Customer insight loop with 70/30 quantitative/qualitative ratio and a monthly synthesis ritual.
- Loyalty and retention plan with onboarding, use, recognition, recovery, advocacy layers and cohort tracking.
- Quarterly business review that ends in budget reallocation, owner, date, and measurable check.
- Lead form and checkout form quality gates derived from form-design research.
- UX writing pattern library: voice chart, 11 patterns, four-phase edit, page-type patterns, microcopy details, error and empty states, regional rules.
- Narrative spine and emotional arc: three-act structure, plot points, emotional arc per stage, red thread, character casting, page sequencing, modular content blocks.
- Ethical persuasion gate: permitted principles, forbidden techniques, audit questions, high-stakes domain rules, behaviour-change discipline, advocacy-led growth.
- Client design decision presentation playbook: meeting design, articulating decisions with trade-offs, listening discipline, response forming, message-by-stakeholder, agreement-locking, follow-up, and change handling.
- Premium discovery protocol with paid-vs-free criteria, hypothesis register, deliverables, validation thresholds, and walk-away rules.
- Post-launch retention playbook: positive and negative signals, relationship rituals, scope expansion conversations, public proof cadence, clean exits.
